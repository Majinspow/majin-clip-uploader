MAJIN Clip Uploader

MAJIN Clip Uploader is a personal desktop tool. It records a Twitch stream the owner has permission to clip, cuts the best moments into vertical short videos and uploads them on a schedule to the owner's own TikTok accounts and the owner's own YouTube channel (Twitch Highlights DE) as YouTube Shorts.

How it works

  1. The owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) once and grants permission to upload videos.
  2. The tool shows the clips it has cut. The owner chooses which clips to publish, the caption and the privacy setting.
  3. The tool uploads each clip through the TikTok Content Posting API, with a pause of several hours between posts.
  4. For YouTube, the owner signs in with Google once (OAuth). Selected clips are uploaded as Shorts to the owner's channel through the YouTube Data API (videos.insert), at most a few per day.

The tool runs only on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to other users.
